## 8. Remaining limitations (stated in the paper)

1. The price model is trained on synthetic data, so real-market accuracy is unknown.
2. The detector labels, the mobile condition options and the location format do not match the price model's vocabulary, so most estimates are unreliable (unresolved).
3. The detector weights are reloaded on every classification request (unresolved); end-to-end latency was not measured.
4. Handover GPS and timestamp come from the device, so this is verification, not proof.
5. The web tests were not re-run; no real-device APK test, Atlas load test, field photo study or user study has been done.
6. The calibrated price interval under-covers on the final period (86.9% against a 90% target) and is not used by the app.
7. Translation quality was not measured, and the mobile app does not use the translation API.
8. The test split shares 5 exact images with the validation split; near-duplicates were not checked.
